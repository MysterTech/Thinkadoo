PAINT MY GOD

MAKE A GANESHA. MAKE A MEMORY.

Festivals often arrive at our doorstep.

  • The flowers are ready.
  • The sweets are ready.
  • The idol is ready.

But what if a child could make a little part of the celebration themselves?

With Paint My God, Ganesha becomes a canvas for little hands and big imaginations.

Paint. Decorate. Experiment.

Discover the colours, symbols and stories woven into a festival we have celebrated for generations.

And when it’s done, there is something more than a Ganesha sitting in front of you.

There is a little piece of the festival that they made themselves.

“I made this.”

WHAT’S INSIDE THE BOX?

Everything you need to create your own Ganesha.

  1. GANESHA IDOL

    A ready-to-paint Ganesha made from child-safe gypsum, waiting for your imagination.

  2. ACRYLIC BRUSH MARKERS

    12 brush-tipped acrylic markers for a mess-free experience.

  3. STICKERS

    A sheet of 6 stickers that are part of the storytelling process.

  4. GEMS & EMBELLISHMENTS

    5 large gems + 5 small gems + 2 flower-shaped gems + 5 assorted gems + 2 googly eyes.

  5. GLUE

    22.5 g bottle for all your pasting.

  6. REVERSIBLE MANTAP PACKAGING

    Flip the packaging to transform it into a little mantap for your finished Ganesha.

  7. COMIC BOOK

    A delightful comic book featuring Ganesha and his vahana, Mushak.

MORE THAN A DIY CRAFT KIT

Paint My God is designed to encourage:

  • CREATIVITY

    Experiment with colours, patterns and ideas.

  • CONCENTRATION

    Slow down and work on something with your hands.

  • FINE MOTOR SKILLS

    Painting, sticking and decorating involve careful hand movements.

  • SELF-EXPRESSION

    There is no single “correct” result.

  • CULTURAL CURIOSITY

    A familiar festival becomes an opportunity to ask questions and discover stories. Most importantly, it creates an experience that children and adults can share.
