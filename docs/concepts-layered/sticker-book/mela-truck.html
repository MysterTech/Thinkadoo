THE GREAT INDIAN MELA TRUCK

BUILD A MELA. MAKE A STORY.

There is something magical about a mela.

  • The bright colours.
  • The little stalls.
  • The games.
  • The noise.
  • The food.
  • The people.

A whole little world appears, and for a few hours, anything can happen.

With Mela Truck, children get to build that world themselves.

  • Put it together.
  • Paint it.
  • Decorate it.
  • Then make up the stories.
  • Who is visiting the mela?
  • What’s happening at the stalls?
  • Where is the truck going next?

There is no single way to play.

You build the mela. You make the story.

WHAT’S INSIDE THE BOX?

7 ACTIVITY ENVELOPES. 7 WAYS TO PLAY.

Each envelope contains everything you need for a different mela activity.

1. FERRIS WHEEL

BUILD · ASSEMBLE · PLAY

Put together your very own working Ferris wheel using the wooden MDF pieces.

Build it. Paint it. Get it turning.

Envelope contains: Pre-cut MDF boards, Passport sticker.

2. SHOOTING GAME

AIM · PULL · HIT

Build your own mini shooting game using paper cores, a rubber-band launcher and a simple trigger mechanism.

Set up the paper-cup targets.

Take aim.

Can you knock them all down?

Envelope contains: 2 paper cores, trigger, 2 rubber bands, end-cap sticker, 6 cups, Passport sticker.

3. LEOPARD HAND PUPPET

SNIP · FOLD · GLUE

Turn a sheet of beautiful prints into a playful leopard hand puppet.

Bring your leopard to life and make up your own stories.

Envelope contains: Leopard print, Passport sticker.

4. KALEIDOSCOPE

MAKE · LOOK · DISCOVER

Build your own kaleidoscope with mirrors, a paper core and a transparent chamber filled with colourful beads and gems.

Turn it.

Look through it.

Watch the world transform.

Envelope contains: Paper core, 3 mirrors, 3 mirror stickers, Passport sticker.

5. STITCH CRAFT

THREAD · WEAVE · CREATE

Thread by thread, weave a little bunny onto a felt sheet.

Add its eyes and nose.

And watch a few simple threads become a character you made yourself.

Envelope contains: Felt, yarn, needle, eyes-and-nose sticker, Passport sticker.

6. CANDY PAINTING

PAINT · DECORATE · IMAGINE

Decorate your very own candy-bar-shaped gypsum pieces with the colours, glue and materials provided.

Make them sweet.

Make them strange.

Make them completely yours.

Envelope contains: 3 gypsum lollipops, glitter, Thermocol case, base sticker, Passport sticker.

7. BUILD YOUR MELA TRUCK

BUILD · ROLL · GO

The box itself becomes the final adventure.

Transform the packaging into your very own Mela Truck using the wheels, axles and drag-string mechanism inside.

Envelope contains: Wheels, axle stick, thread, Passport sticker.

Load up your imagination and take your mela wherever you go.

SEVEN ACTIVITIES. ONE BIG ADVENTURE.

Mela Truck is designed to take children through different kinds of making.

  • BUILD.
  • AIM.
  • FOLD.
  • DISCOVER.
  • STITCH.
  • PAINT.
  • PLAY.

Each activity is different.

Each one asks them to use their hands in a new way.

MORE THAN A DIY TOY

Mela Truck is designed to encourage:

  • CREATIVITY

    Make choices. Experiment. Decorate. Invent.

  • IMAGINATIVE PLAY

    Create characters, stories and adventures around your mela.

  • PROBLEM SOLVING

    Build mechanisms, follow instructions and figure things out.

  • FINE MOTOR SKILLS

    Cutting, folding, threading, painting and assembling all involve careful hands-on work.

  • CURIOSITY

    Each activity introduces a different material, process or little piece of discovery.

And perhaps most importantly:

It gives children something to do, not just something to watch.
