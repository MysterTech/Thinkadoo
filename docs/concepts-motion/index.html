A whole lot of doing.

What to review

Look at how each world moves, then follow its separate Shop, Workshops, Our story and Contact pages. Compare how quickly you understand the offer, how the making gesture feels, and whether practical information stays easy to reach across page transitions.

The original Thinkadoo wordmarks and all seven brand colours are preserved. Illustrations are newly authored concept art. No real product or workshop video was supplied, so the moving sequences are labelled illustrated previews.
