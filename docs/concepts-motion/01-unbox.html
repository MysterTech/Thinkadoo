What to expect

Come curious.

You don't need to know how to draw. You don't need to be "creative". You don't need previous experience. Just bring your curiosity.

Make with your hands.

Everything you need is provided. We teach you the process, then give you room to make it your own.

Meet other makers.

Thinkadoo workshops are deliberately small. Because creativity is more interesting when people talk, exchange ideas and see how differently everyone approaches the same thing.

Take something home.

Every workshop ends with something you've made. And wherever possible, you can take a Thinkadoo kit home and continue making.

Learn → Make → Take home → Make again

WITH YOUR HANDS.

Today you can access almost anything instantly. A picture. A video. A game. An answer.

But there are some things a screen cannot give you.

The resistance of paper.
The smell of paint.
The satisfaction of fitting two pieces together.

The surprise of discovering that your hands can make something you didn't know how to make five minutes ago.

We want to bring more of that back. Not by taking you away from the modern world. But by giving you more ways to experience it.

About

Gayathri Jeaks
& Girish Madhavan

Thinkadoo is founded by Gayathri Jeaks and Girish Madhavan, designers and storytellers with backgrounds in animation, communication design, film and visual storytelling.

Gayathri and Girish are graduates of IIT Bombay's postgraduate design programmes, with Gayathri specialising in Animation and Film Design and Girish in Communication Design.

Our work has always been about communicating ideas through images, stories, objects and experiences.

Thinkadoo brings that thinking into the world of children's creativity, DIY crafts, cultural toys and hands-on experiences.

We believe a more creative generation can build a more thoughtful world.
