What to expect

Come curious.

You don’t need to know how to draw, be “creative”, or have previous experience. Just bring your curiosity.

Make with your hands.

Everything you need is provided. We teach you the process, then give you room to make it your own.

Meet other makers.

Our workshops are deliberately small. Talk, exchange ideas and see how differently everyone approaches the same thing.

Take something home.

Every workshop ends with something you’ve made. Wherever possible, take a corresponding Thinkadoo kit home and continue making.

Why we make

The world feels different.With your hands.

Today you can access almost anything instantly. A picture. A video. A game. An answer.

But there are some things a screen cannot give you.

The resistance of paper.
The smell of paint.
The satisfaction of fitting two pieces together.

The surprise of discovering that your hands can make something you didn’t know how to make five minutes ago.

We want to bring more of that back. Not by taking you away from the modern world. But by giving you more ways to experience it. WITH YOUR HANDS.

Meet Gayathri & Girish

Thinkadoo is founded by Gayathri Jeaks and Girish Madhavan, designers and storytellers with backgrounds in animation, communication design, film and visual storytelling.

Both are graduates of IIT Bombay’s postgraduate design programmes. Gayathri specialised in Animation and Film Design, and Girish in Communication Design.

Our work has always been about communicating ideas through images, stories, objects and experiences. Thinkadoo brings that thinking into children’s creativity, DIY crafts, cultural toys and hands-on experiences.

We believe a more creative generation can build a more thoughtful world.
