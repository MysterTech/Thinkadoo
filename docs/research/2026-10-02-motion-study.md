## Kinder Praxis & Klinik

https://www.kinder-und-klinik.de/

At 1440 × 1000 the opening is an enormous blue face. Its eyes contain independently moving pupils. Natural wheel scroll of 800px changed the .mouth region from a 20px height to 500px, while the eye groups changed their translation. The next section is literally the expanding mouth; section transition and illustration are one mechanism. Earlier source inspection identifies a tooth dropping/rotating and photos expanding from circles into rectangles. The nav remains conventional. Thinkadoo transfer: make packaging/folded paper open into the next useful section, instead of attaching a wobbling sticker to a conventional section.

## React.gg

https://react.gg/

A thick edged tactile card deck occupies the hero. Pointer down/move/up on the stack was exercised; screenshots capture the exposed arrangement (text readback cannot measure these image-only cards). The learning section contains a real input toggle. Activating it changed the component diagram DOM; the earlier visit visually verified Traditional/React arrangement differences. The curved learning path makes separate topics feel part of a physical journey. Transfer: allow direct manipulation of envelopes or stamp materials, then provide a stable button alternative. Motion must explain the object and leave a useful, inspectable state.

## Locker Land

https://www.lockerland.com.au/

The initial red composition settles into a curved carousel of large tilted cards. It auto-advances, has step controls and announces its playback state. Hover on the shopper link changed its computed transform from none to matrix(1.05, 0, 0, 1.05, 0, -8). Selecting a lower shopper card opened a real dialog with more detail and a next-persona route. Scrolling moves from playful oversized objects to practical information and a locker door used for progressive disclosure. Source inspection in the earlier study confirms quadratic placement, spring hover, pointer capture and snap. Transfer: use material-shaped navigation and a substantial product browsing rail; click targets should be stable and useful after movement stops.

## Poptastic

https://poptasticpop.com/

The hero anchors one enormous product object over multiple large text tracks. During a roughly one-second interval plus wheel scroll, ticker translation changed from about -100px to -150px. The product stays foreground while text supplies continuous background energy. At scroll 1000px the page changes to a product detail composition using tilted packaging, a scalloped information shape and a contrasting colour field. Earlier interaction advanced its product shelf with a next arrow. Transfer: commit to truly large typography/object scale and independent layers. Avoid six equally animated lines competing with essential copy. Keep the readable headline, actions and prices stable.

## HappiLoop

https://happiloop-slabpixel.webflow.io/

The load resolves into opposing tilted headline halves, a giant bottle and several illustrated characters. Pointer movement visibly changes depth: headline matrices gained about 2px of translation while supporting groups gained about 3.4px in this sample. Earlier source inspection documents larger product/parallax ranges, back-eased scaling, magnetic button hooks, rotating arrow hovers and opposed 12-second strips. Scroll 850px revealed the scalloped transition and large next-section typography; content falls into calmer readable areas. Transfer: orchestrate the entrance of type and object, then give sections distinct rhythms. Mobile should be independently recomposed, and every apparent control must function.

## Design decisions

The four new hypotheses are Unbox a little universe (packaging unfolds), Type is a toy (kinetic type and puppet/carousel), A world in a fold (large paper geometry becomes a creative scene), and The print club (direct stamping and printed text). They differ in primary material, camera, alignment, motion trigger and useful output. All have real product details, workshop previews, local cart, story/community/contact routes and controllable motion.

Review captures must record changes at two scroll positions and before/after a signature interaction. Counting CSS animations or collecting a single screenshot does not establish behaviour. Use natural scroll, no hijacking; manual alternatives on touch and keyboard; media-query and explicit motion-off freeze CSS, scroll and pointer effects. Customer copy stays readable and controls remain stable.
