Website Visual Direction
Overall feel: loud, tactile, and joyful, like opening a kit. Playful but tasteful, not a typical pastel kids' site.
Audience: appeals to parents with good taste while delighting kids.
Typography: oversized, heavy, tightly set headlines, often full-width. Chunky rounded or display sans-serifs. Words stack, overlap shapes, and break across lines. Body text is clean and simple.
Colour: bold, flat, and saturated, with no gradients.
Shapes: big geometric forms (circles, arches, scallops, stars, blobs)
Motion: buttons squish or bounce on hover, stickers wobble, marquee text strips scroll, and sections pop in on scroll. Playful, not chaotic.

ABOUT

Today you can access almost anything instantly.
A picture.
 A video.
 A game.
 An answer.
But there are some things a screen cannot give you.
The resistance of paper.
The smell of paint.
The satisfaction of fitting two pieces together.
The surprise of discovering that your hands can make something you didn't know how to make five minutes ago.
We want to bring more of that back.
Not by taking you away from the modern world.
But by giving you more ways to experience it.
WITH YOUR HANDS.



Thinkadoo is founded by Gayathri Jeaks and Girish Madhavan, designers and storytellers with backgrounds in animation, communication design, film and visual storytelling.
Gayathri and Girish are graduates of IIT Bombay's postgraduate design programmes, with Gayathri specialising in Animation and Film Design and Girish in Communication Design.
Our work has always been about communicating ideas through images, stories, objects and experiences.
Thinkadoo brings that thinking into the world of children's creativity, DIY crafts, cultural toys and hands-on experiences.
We believe a more creative generation can build a more thoughtful world.
