# Visual and behavioral reference study

Inspected in the in-app browser on 27–28 September 2026. Public source inspection complements screenshots and interactions; source declarations are not represented as performance measurements. The brief is [Website Design Notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Z4dyktCG_hYdKzRoZ1Gg_AuqCDDCeY2_XpU0DxUDFrg/edit). [Brand folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1T93IFRpyeTYLJG3d2sxmIQPdyVS7yb7w) contains two transparent wordmarks and the rejected HTML concept.

## Kinder Praxis & Klinik

[Reference](https://www.kinder-und-klinik.de/). Observed desktop and narrow viewport. The first viewport is an enormous friendly face occupying a cobalt field; the next cyan region becomes its toothy mouth, then reveals the large welcome text. This is environmental identity, not a conventional illustrated hero. Gilroy is the computed typeface; welcome display measured 108px at the inspected desktop viewport, later headings 49.5px. Bright shapes have defined jobs as face parts and page boundaries. A single appointment action is kept simple. Lower content becomes more textual: services, age groups, spaces, people and parent FAQ. Rounded stacked service panels connect the joyful opening to adult information. The mobile face retains its readable silhouette. Transfer: let kit materials form the stage and connect sections; keep conventional routes to practical decisions.

## react.gg

[Reference](https://react.gg/). Dark gridded ground, off-white outlines and saturated collectable-card faces. The initial stack is an actual draggable deck: dragging the blender card away revealed the next retro computer card. Below it, a large winding course-track graphic connects discrete content chapters. The Traditional/React toggle was exercised and visibly changed the component diagram's arrangement; this is an explanation embedded in marketing. A later quiz deck repeats the same material interaction in a new content job. Key transfer: one manipulation explains the product. Thinkadoo can unpack, print or construct, then use the same mechanism through the page. Do not import the black technical grid or course claims automatically.

## Locker Land

[Reference](https://www.lockerland.com.au/). Loaded successfully in the browser although the text retrieval tool initially failed. An opening red field builds into a centered wordmark and a curved, rotating rail of tilted navigation cards. A header chapter navigation appears in the settled page. Cards link to shoppers, action, merchant/host benefits and downloadable guides. Scroll sections use checked bands and large object metaphors. Shopper expansion opened a detailed overlay with stacked card imagery, close control and next-persona route. A locker action opened an explanatory host-benefit overlay. This translates a physical object into navigation and progressive disclosure. During automation an animated shopper target yielded a different persona; do not claim flawless interaction behavior. Transfer: purposeful hinges/chapters and grounded navigation, with stable click targets and explicit alternative controls.

## Poptastic

[Reference](https://poptasticpop.com/). Red opening saturated to the edges, many large moving lines of type behind one oversized real popcorn bowl. Product remains the foreground anchor. A simple contrasting CTA leads to the assortment. Scroll moves into a large single-product feature—tilted pack beside a scalloped paper information field—then an open horizontal shelf on a different colour. Next-arrow interaction visibly advanced the product rail. Product cards contain real quantities, pricing and cart actions; those facts are specific to Poptastic and must not be borrowed. A timed email popup interrupted research and was dismissed. Transfer: colour regions, scale and tactile packaging can create energy without boxing every section. Avoid copying the unsolicited popup and excessive competing text motion.

## HappiLoop

[Reference](https://happiloop-slabpixel.webflow.io/). An orchestrated load changes an almost empty purple stage into huge split lettering, smiling geometric characters, a large tilted vitamin jar and handwritten direction arrows. Computed display is Narnoor, 290px on the inspected desktop viewport; section type is 96px. Scalloped/curved boundaries connect sections. Source declares hero scale from zero, initial navigation offsets, several opacity entries, 3D hover wrappers and magnetic-button hooks. Below, colourful character/product panels and crossing marquees establish a repeatable world. The four small bottle images look selectable, but clicking one did not visibly change the main bottle during inspection. Many nav links are hash placeholders. Transfer: large meaningful object, typography behind it, cohesive characters and staggered entrances; improve semantics, visible defaults and actual controls instead of copying template incompleteness. Narrow viewport review exposed large transitional gaps; our mobile compositions must be independently designed.

## Synthesis for Thinkadoo

The common thread is authored physical behavior, not merely saturated colours. Product objects explain the brand. Typography changes scale boldly. Geometry frames images and transitions. Motion follows the objects' imagined materials. Calm text follows spectacle so adults can decide. Four concepts must differ in camera, composition, rhythm and primary manipulation—not just palette. Actual reference illustrations and photos are not copied.

For review, compare whether a parent can identify the offer and action, whether a child can enjoy the visual mechanism, whether motion remains controllable, and whether product/story/workshop content remains straightforward after the first impression. The resulting prototypes test design hypotheses; they are not user research evidence.

## Source-defined behavior details

An independent read-only pass downloaded directly referenced public CSS/JS to /private/tmp/thinkadoo-reference-analysis. Manifest maps files to source URLs. These timings are declarations, not measured frame-rate or interaction latency.

- React: spinner hover turns 1440 degrees in 1.2s; game-piece body bobs 0.2em while shadow shrinks to 0.94 over 0.8s; button shadow transition is 150ms and covers focus-visible. Atom spins over 10s; reduced-motion rule found stops atom but does not establish complete coverage. Sources: https://react.gg/_astro/e.CTkUsz55.css and https://react.gg/_astro/index.BnP43qHa.css.
- Locker Land: curved rail derives vertical placement from a quadratic curve, default 44px curvature, 6-degree tilt and 1.12 center scale. Hover scale 1.05 and lift 8px use spring stiffness 400/damping 17; stepping takes 300ms cubic ease-out. Pointer capture and velocity lead to snap. Reduced motion disables autoplay/spin-in. In-view headings move 24px over 0.5s, text follows after 0.25s. Sticky navigation is verified in source; whole-page pinning is not. Source: https://www.lockerland.com.au/_next/static/chunks/0av-jxxqkou_j.js?dpl=dpl_88z3gqsAKi37tvjZWTcVhrCkMorw.
- HappiLoop: headline scale 0–1 over 600ms outBack, rotations to -6/+6.3 degrees over 800ms; product pop 700–800ms. Arrow hover rotates 22 degrees over 300ms outSine. Opposed strips traverse 1736.7px over 12s. Desktop pointer depth: product ±20px, arrows ±10px, text ±5px, headline ±3px. Most entrances trigger on entering viewport, not scrub. FlowBitz magnetic-button hook is present; its external implementation wasn't audited. Source: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67bc268acc8e91a501037ff7/js/webflow.3bea0ee7.31b758a947d5a792.js.
- Poptastic: ticker moves one segment width continuously at about 50px/s; floating icon combines a 400ms bounce entrance with -50% to 0% vertical scrub over one viewport. Sources: https://poptasticpop.com/cdn/shop/t/21/assets/tickertape-rows.js and https://poptasticpop.com/cdn/shop/t/21/assets/floating-icon.js.
- Kinder: mouth height is tied to scroll; tooth drops and rotates after passing viewport midpoint; photos scrub from circular 80% size to full rectangles. Smooth scrolling is disabled with reduced motion but separate effects still register. Source: https://www.kinder-und-klinik.de/assets/dist/js/main.js?v1.6.

Use the principles, not the exact numbers as cargo-cult tokens: deliberate manipulation (React), physically arranged objects (Locker Land), orchestrated entrance (HappiLoop), background energy (Poptastic), scroll transforms the illustration (Kinder). Keep Thinkadoo motion fully controllable across every effect.
