# Design System: Thinkadoo exploration

## Overview

The supplied identity is confident, physical and irregular: a jagged outlined wordmark, stars, saturated colours, and an invitation to make. Four worlds are being explored; none is selected for production. The source contract lives in website-concepts.md. Mode for all four homepages: Persuade, with optional moments of Experience.

## Colors

Preserve the source primitives. Commit large fields to a dominant palette per direction. Paper neutrals support objects and readable content; avoid making all four predominantly cream. Deep blue or ink on yellow, teal and green generally provides the body-text pairing. Verify actual contrast instead of assuming white works on every bright colour. No gradients.

## Typography

Maker's table: Bricolage Grotesque and Manrope. Playroom: Fredoka and a simple body sans. Craft journal: Archivo Black and Archivo. Paper theatre: Baloo 2 and Nunito Sans. Keep real logo artwork separate from display typography. Oversized lettering is intentional and source-mandated; responsive limits and line breaks must preserve comprehension. Avoid tracked tiny labels and repetitive eyebrows.

## Layout

Four independently composed first viewports: overhead table; centered interactive toy shelf; asymmetric typographic/art spread; panoramic cut-paper stage. Scroll should alternate spectacle with useful calm, not repeat the same card grid and spacing. Conventional top navigation remains immediately available. At small sizes, reorder and recompose rather than shrink an entire desktop canvas. Primary actions stay readable and reachable.

## Elevation & Depth

Use depth when it conveys material: paper layers, folded tabs, objects lifted from a tabletop, a hinged curtain. Prefer crisp or short soft cast shadows to generic floating cards. No glass effects or neon halos.

## Shapes

Source shapes include circles, arches, scallops, stars and organic cutouts. Shapes must depict kit materials or carry a job—label, aperture, stage edge, stamp—not fill arbitrary gaps. Heavy outlines echo the logo where they suit the particular world.

## Components

Buttons visibly press, tilt, ink or unfold according to their world. Hover never carries essential information; focus and touch receive the same outcome. Product details can use a native dialog with Escape, close, trapped focus and restoration. Workshop previews show sample activity choices and honest unavailability. Navigation anchors work; footer policy/contact actions explain missing content. A subtle review control and Motion toggle are the only prototype controls.

Motion is authored around one main mechanism per concept: assembly, mode change, printing, or theatre chapters. Add secondary motion only to support that mechanism. Respect reduced motion and the global toggle across CSS and JS. Decorative loops pause when off-screen; content never begins universally hidden. No scroll hijacking.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use actual logo PNGs with their original aspect and stars.
- Do give product objects space and visual specificity.
- Do make every obvious affordance work locally.
- Do label conceptual art and provisional product content on the review board and details.
- Don't copy the rejected homepage composition or slogan stack.
- Don't borrow the references' commercial claims, mascots, product photography or brand art.
- Don't invent counts, reviews, dates, scarcity, contact addresses or policies.
- Don't confuse a visual prototype with a selected architecture.
