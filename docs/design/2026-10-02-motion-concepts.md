### Interaction purpose and scenic layering — revised after review

The useful quality in the two positive references is the composition of three or four coherent object planes into one scene: backdrop, environmental middle layer, principal object or character, and a foreground frame or tool. Depth should create a delightful place to explore, rather than a pile of independently moving decorations.

Every concept has one primary scenic system. Its motion tells a clear making story, and every moving element must reveal content, show how an object works, preserve spatial hierarchy, respond to a direct manipulation, or provide visible feedback. Remove continuous drift, text motion, parallax, hover rotation and entrance effects that do not serve one of those purposes. Content sections may remain still; the scene earns the motion.

Each scene uses three or four visibly distinct planes with unequal depth. The backdrop establishes the room or world and moves least. The middle layer establishes the activity. The principal object carries the interaction and receives the clearest motion. A foreground frame, pair of hands, curtain, workbench edge or tool creates intimacy and moves only when it helps open or close the scene. Shadows, overlaps, cropped edges and occlusion make depth legible even when motion is disabled.

Scroll is chapter based. A short sticky scene may progress through two to four authored states, with stable resting compositions between them. It must not feel like separate layers move merely because scroll data is available. Hover/focus belongs only on objects that look touchable; its response previews the same action click/tap completes. Text stays readable and generally still, except when the text itself is the manipulated material and the change has a defined meaning. Reduced-motion mode shows the final layered composition without losing content or controls.

## Motion and accessibility rules

Use natural passive scroll with at most one requestAnimationFrame-bound update per page; work only while the relevant stage is visible. Bounded transforms, no scroll hijacking, sounds or custom cursor. Fine-pointer depth disables on coarse pointer. A single scene can have an entrance; avoid every section doing the same generic fade/slide. Primary actions and body text stay readable/stable, no essential content starts universally hidden. Off-screen loops pause; auto rails pause on hover/focus and expose play/pause. User Motion toggle and prefers-reduced-motion freeze all CSS animation, JS scroll and pointer transforms; root smooth scrolling also disables. Common module sets html[data-motion="off|on"] and dispatches thinkadoo:motion with detail.enabled. Concept listens and resolves to complete static state. Media-query changes remain live. Manual controls work instantly in reduced mode.

Visible focus, semantic buttons/labels/aria states, native details/dialogs; dialog Escape, close and focus restoration. Scrollable modal max height. No body/document overflow masking to hide mobile defects. Local animation wrappers may clip their decorative layers. Check 1440×1000, 390×844 and 360px width. No external integrations or animation libraries; Google Fonts with fallback permitted. Shared local assets mean static server or file URLs both work.
