## Platform

web

## Users

Parents exploring hands-on activities for children; children are participants and an emotional audience. The 2 October content brief also welcomes curious adults to creative workshops and invites schools, communities, cafés, bookstores, cultural spaces, companies, festivals and art communities as hosts. Retailers and collaborators have explicit contact paths.

## Product Purpose

User-confirmed definition: “Thinkadoo is a children's craft-kit and workshop brand that turns imagination, art and creative discovery into screen-free hands-on experiences for children.” The public website helps families understand, explore and eventually purchase kits or book workshops. This task creates four working visual prototypes for comparison, not the final service.

## Positioning

Lead with imagination, art and creative discovery through screen-free hands-on experiences. Indian traditions can be one source of individual kit content, not an exclusive definition of the brand. The two example kit names and their details remain provisional content inherited from the concept. Do not frame Thinkadoo as solely a heritage or cultural-traditions brand.

## Brand Commitments

Use the provided red/yellow Thinkadoo wordmarks unchanged and the seven source palette colours. Bold, flat, saturated, tactile and joyful; parent appeal and child delight. Oversized expressive sans-serif headlines, geometric forms, playful controlled motion, no gradients. Study all five referenced websites for behavior as well as appearance.

## Product Principles

- Show the making activity and its resulting object.
- Keep parent decisions clear while making exploration enjoyable.
- Every presented control gives understandable, reversible feedback.
- Preserve the brand; explore materially different compositions and behaviors.
